# Aurora Outfitters — 2028 Sustainability Impact Report

*Year three of the Carbon-Negative by 2030 commitment*

---

## Letter from the field

Three years ago Aurora made a public commitment: Carbon-Negative by 2030. This report is year-three accountability. We are not on a glide path — we are mid-flight, with material wins and a clear-eyed view of what remains.

The most important number in this report is **18 kg of CO2-equivalent avoided per Repair-for-Life repair completed**. We completed 14,200 repairs in 2028. That is 255,600 kg of CO2-equivalent avoided in a single year through repair alone. Repair is our largest lever, and it scales with Trail Club enrolment growth, which is up 41% year-over-year.

— *Aurora Outfitters, 2028 Annual Disclosure*

## Repair programme metrics

**14,200 repairs completed in 2028:**

- 6,800 sole replacements (overwhelmingly Aurora TerraTrek 7, with Helios approach boots in second place)
- 3,100 zipper replacements (Cumulus 2P and Skyline 45L)
- 2,400 down re-lofting (Helios jacket and Quill 850)
- 1,200 seam re-seals
- 700 strap and buckle replacements (Crag XR and Skyline 45L)

**Estimated avoided emissions:** 255,600 kg CO2-equivalent. This is roughly equivalent to the annual energy use of 52 typical US households, or 65 round-trip transatlantic economy flights.

**Per-repair turnaround averages:**

- Trail Club Standard: 9.2 business days (SLA: 10)
- Trail Club Plus: 4.6 business days (SLA: 5)
- Trail Club Pro: 1.8 business days (SLA: 2)

Every tier is inside SLA. The Portland and Annecy repair centres operated at 84% and 78% capacity utilisation respectively — there is room to absorb 2029 growth without facility expansion.

## Ambassador travel offsets

All travel by Aurora's four ambassadors — Mara Chen, Jonah Reyes, Dr Anya Patel, and Theo Sundberg — is carbon-offset at 1.5× the calculated footprint via direct purchase of removal credits (not avoidance credits).

**2028 ambassador travel footprint:** 84 tCO2e gross, 126 tCO2e offset, net -42 tCO2e.

A quote from Mara Chen on the 2028 field season: *"60 days of testing across Tasmania, Patagonia, and the Tour du Mont Blanc. The flights are unavoidable for the work. What is avoidable is the equipment churn — and that is what Repair-for-Life solves at scale. Every boot I repair is a boot a customer doesn't replace."*

A quote from Jonah Reyes on the Continental Divide Trail thru-hike: *"3,100 miles in the Aurora Quill 850 and Aurora Skyline 45L. End of the season the pack went back to Portland for a hip-belt re-seal and shipped back inside the Pro tier two-day SLA. That product is going another five years."*

## Supplier audits

Aurora audits its top 12 suppliers annually against the Higg Index Facility Environmental Module (FEM). 2028 results:

- **11 of 12 suppliers** met or exceeded 75th-percentile FEM scores.
- **1 supplier** (a leather tannery in Tuscany) is on a 12-month improvement plan after scoring at the 58th percentile. We are not delisting; we are co-funding the wastewater-treatment upgrade. Outcome will be reported in the 2029 disclosure.

---

## What's open

The honest list of what we have not solved:

1. **Scope 3 supplier decarbonisation.** Slowest-moving lever. 2029 priority is co-funded transition for the top-4 emitters.
2. **End-of-life recycling pathways for the Crag XR harness.** Climbing harnesses are notoriously hard to recycle once retired. We are in early R&D with a Catalonia-based facility on a chemical-recovery process for the recycled-polyester webbing.
3. **The 2030 net-negative position depends on removal-credit availability.** We have an option-contract structure with three direct-air-capture providers, but pricing volatility is real. Disclosure of contract pricing will appear in the 2029 report.

## Brand pillar alignment

This report is the year-three accountability checkpoint on two of the four Aurora brand pillars:

- **Repairable for Life** — operationalised through the Trail Club programme and the repair centres in Portland and Annecy.
- **Carbon-Negative by 2030** — the 2030 target remains feasible at current trajectory; Scope 3 is the open work.

The other two pillars — *Built for the Worst Weather* and *Trail-Tested by Experts* — are reinforced in this work, not separate from it. A repaired Aurora TerraTrek 7 used by Mara Chen across three destinations is the worst-weather, expert-tested, lifespan-extended product. The pillars compose.
