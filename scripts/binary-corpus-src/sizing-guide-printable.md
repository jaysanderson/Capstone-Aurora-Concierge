# Aurora Outfitters — Sizing Guide

*Printable reference for boots, packs, and harnesses. Bring this to a fitting session or use it at home.*

---

## How to measure

You need a tape measure, a pencil, and a sheet of paper larger than your foot.

1. Place the paper on a hard floor against a wall.
2. Stand on the paper with your heel against the wall.
3. Trace the outline of your foot.
4. Measure heel-to-longest-toe in centimetres.
5. Measure the widest point of the forefoot in centimetres.

Do this for **both feet**. They are rarely the same. Use the larger of the two for boot sizing.

---

## Sizing profiles

Aurora boots are built around four sizing profiles. Each profile is a last shape — the underlying mould the boot is built on. **Pick the profile that matches your foot anatomy first; then pick a size.**

| Profile | Forefoot width (cm) | Arch | Best fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Standard** | 9.5 – 10.2 (men) / 8.7 – 9.4 (women) | Medium | The majority of customers |
| **Wide Foot** | 10.3+ (men) / 9.5+ (women) | Medium-low | Customers historically in 2E or wider |
| **Narrow Foot** | Below 9.5 (men) / 8.7 (women) | Medium | Slim heel, slim forefoot |
| **High Arch** | Standard width | High | Customers who slide forward on descents in standard boots |

If you're uncertain between Standard and Wide Foot, **book a fitting with Dr Anya Patel via Trail Club Plus** — Aurora ambassadors do dedicated sizing-profile assessments for Trail Club members.

## Pack sizing (Aurora Skyline 45L)

Pack size is **torso length**, not chest or waist. Measure from the C7 vertebra (the bony bump at the base of your neck when you tilt forward) to the iliac crest (top of your hip bone, level with your belly button).

| Torso length (cm) | Skyline 45L size |
|---|---|
| 40 – 45 | XS |
| 45 – 50 | S |
| 50 – 55 | M |
| 55 – 60 | L |

Hip-belt sizing is independent and adjustable across all torso sizes. Aurora packs ship with a 70 – 110 cm hip-belt range standard.

## Harness sizing (Aurora Crag XR — Trail Club Plus exclusive)

Crag XR is built on a single adjustable architecture. Sized by waist + leg-loop combination.

| Waist (cm) | Leg loop (cm) | Crag XR size |
|---|---|---|
| 70 – 80 | 47 – 55 | S |
| 78 – 92 | 50 – 60 | M |
| 88 – 102 | 55 – 65 | L |
| 98 – 112 | 60 – 70 | XL |

## When to come in for a fitting

You should book a fitting if:

- Your forefoot width sits between profiles.
- You're transitioning between activity types (e.g. Weekend Adventurer → Thru-Hiker).
- You've had on-trail foot injury you suspect is fit-related.
- You're sizing a child or a customer with mobility considerations.

**Trail Club Plus and Pro tiers include unlimited fitting sessions.** Trail Club Standard members get one free fitting per year.
