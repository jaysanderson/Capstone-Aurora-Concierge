# Aurora Outfitters — Warranty and Repair Policy

*Official policy document. Effective 1 January 2028. Supersedes all prior warranty terms.*

---

## 1. Coverage scope

Aurora Outfitters warranties every product against defects in materials and workmanship for the **functional lifespan of the product**, defined as the period over which the product can be repaired or restored to its intended performance. This is in line with the **Repairable for Life** brand pillar and is independent of any consumer-protection minimum imposed in your region of purchase.

Functional lifespan is product-specific:

| Product | Expected functional lifespan |
|---|---|
| Aurora TerraTrek 7 (boot) | 8 – 12 years with timely repair |
| Aurora Helios (jacket) | 10 – 15 years |
| Aurora Skyline 45L (pack) | 12 – 20 years |
| Aurora Cumulus 2P / Stratus 1P (tents) | 15 – 25 years |
| Aurora Quill 850 (quilt) | 20+ years with periodic re-loft |
| Aurora Crag XR (harness) | 7 years from date of manufacture (climbing-equipment safety standard) |

## 2. Repair-for-Life programme

The Repair-for-Life programme is Aurora's primary warranty fulfilment mechanism. Customers may submit any Aurora product for repair regardless of tier — costs and turnaround vary by **Trail Club** tier:

| Trail Club tier | Annual repair allowance | Per-repair cost (above allowance) | Turnaround SLA |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Standard** | 1 free repair per product, lifetime | At-cost parts + 30% labour | 10 business days |
| **Plus** | First repair per product per year free | At-cost parts + 15% labour | 5 business days |
| **Pro** | Unlimited free repairs | Free | 2 business days |

Trail Club Pro members also receive priority routing through the Portland and Annecy repair centres and may book ambassador-led fitting sessions to confirm post-repair fit.

## 3. Claims process

To submit a repair claim:

1. Log into your Aurora account and open the **Trail Club** portal.
2. Select **Submit a repair** and identify the product. Aurora stocks SKU-linked records of every original purchase; you will not be asked for a receipt.
3. Describe the failure mode. Photos help but are not required.
4. Choose your preferred repair centre (Portland for Americas / Asia-Pacific, Annecy for EMEA).
5. Print the prepaid shipping label and ship the product.
6. Receive status updates at each stage: received, assessed, in-repair, shipped.

Customers without Trail Club enrolment may still submit repairs; pricing is at standard at-cost rates with no SLA guarantee. Trail Club Standard enrolment is free and is the recommended path for any repeat customer.

## 4. Free-repair triggers (covered without question)

Aurora's standard policy is to repair free of charge, regardless of tier or annual allowance, when the failure mode is in this list:

- **Sole separation on a TerraTrek 7 or Helios approach boot within the first 24 months of purchase**, regardless of mileage.
- **Zipper failure on any Cumulus or Stratus tent** within 5 years of purchase.
- **Down loss on any Helios jacket or Quill 850 quilt** exceeding 20% of original fill weight at routine inspection.
- **Stitching failure on any Skyline 45L hip-belt seam** within the functional lifespan window.
- **Any harness webbing failure** on a Crag XR within the 7-year safety-rated period.

These are observed common failure modes the engineering team has determined are not consumer-caused. Submitting one of these failures does not draw down your annual allowance.

## 5. Exclusions

The following are not covered under warranty:

- Damage caused by deliberate misuse or modification (e.g., cutting straps, removing safety hardware on a Crag XR).
- Cosmetic wear that does not affect function (scuffs, faded dye, weathered patina).
- Loss or theft of the product.
- Damage from rodent or insect chewing (we acknowledge this happens; we will quote a repair but not free of charge).
- Damage to a Crag XR after the 7-year safety-rated period — at end of safety-rated life, the harness must be retired and may not be repaired and returned to climbing use.

## 6. Climbing-equipment safety note (Aurora Crag XR)

The **Aurora Crag XR** is a life-safety product. In line with UIAA and EN 12277 standards, the harness has a maximum 7-year service life from the date of manufacture, regardless of repair status. Aurora will repair Crag XR products within this period under the Trail Club programme but will not return a repaired Crag XR to a customer beyond the 7-year window. End-of-life Crag XR products may be returned to Aurora for **end-of-life material recovery** at no cost — see Section 9.

## 7. Repair turnaround SLAs in detail

| Tier | Standard turnaround | Peak season (June – September) |
|---|---|---|
| Trail Club Standard | 10 business days | 14 business days |
| Trail Club Plus | 5 business days | 7 business days |
| Trail Club Pro | 2 business days | 2 business days (held to SLA) |

If Aurora exceeds the SLA for your tier, you receive **a credit equal to 10% of the original product price** applied to your Trail Club account, on top of the repair being completed at no cost.

## 9. End-of-life material recovery

Aurora accepts any retired Aurora product for material recovery at no cost. Ship to either repair centre with **End-of-Life Recovery** marked on the box. Returned products are disassembled and the materials enter Aurora's closed-loop recycling stream where possible. This service is independent of Trail Club tier and is open to any customer regardless of where the product was purchased.
