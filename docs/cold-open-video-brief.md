# Aurora Concierge — Cold Open · Video Brief

## What this is

This is a 2-minute, no-dialogue cold open that plays in-room immediately before Jay opens the browser for the live Aurora Concierge demo. The lights dim, the video runs, the lights come up, and Jay is already at the keyboard. We need it because the live demo opens on the same web surface the audience has seen a thousand times — and within 8 minutes Jay has to make them feel that this one is fundamentally different. Words can't do that on their own. Contrast does. The video shows the dead-eyed commerce experience every buyer already lives inside, then snaps to Aurora's surfaces moving with intent. By the time the screen cuts to black on the tagline, the audience should already be leaning forward, quietly pulling for Jay to be right — before he has said a single word.

## Total length and format

- Runtime: **1:50–2:10**
- Aspect: **16:9**, **1080p minimum** (deliver 4K master if available)
- Container: **mp4 / H.264**, plus a **ProRes 422 HQ master**
- **No dialogue.** Music + on-screen text only.
- Music brief: alpine cinematic build — see "Music direction" below.
- Audio: stereo, mastered to **-16 LUFS** integrated for room playback (see checklist).

## Music direction

The score does the emotional heavy lifting. We need a build, not a banger. Think trailer-for-a-prestige-documentary, not action-movie-drop.

**Reference tracks** (for tone — not for licensing):
- Hans Zimmer — *Time* (Inception). The slow, inevitable build. Use as the structural reference for shots 5–10.
- Ólafur Arnalds — *Near Light* or *Saman*. Use as the texture reference for shots 1–4 (sparse piano, room-tone intimacy).
- Bonobo — *Cirrus* (instrumental, no vocals). Use for the rhythmic pulse under shots 7–9.

**Royalty-free equivalents** to source on **Artlist** or **Musicbed**:
- Artlist: search "cinematic alpine build", "neo-classical piano emotional", "documentary score crescendo". Look at composers like *Borrtex*, *Stanley Gurvich*, *Kingdom of Antlers*.
- Musicbed: filter by *Cinematic > Building > Inspiring* and *Neo-Classical*. Look for tracks tagged "trailer" with a runtime ≥ 2:30 so the editor has tail to work with.

**Tempo curve:**

| Section | Shots | Feel | Instrumentation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sparse | 1–4 | Cold, airless, slightly off-putting | Solo piano, hollow synth pad, room tone |
| Beat | 5–6 | Suspended. Question hanging in the air. | Silence + single bass thumps + sub-rumble |
| Build | 7–8 | Warmth re-enters. Pulse arrives. | Cello, heartbeat bass, light felt percussion |
| Peak | 9–10 | Full alpine cinematic. Resolves on homepage. | Full strings, percussion, shimmer top end, sustained major chord resolve |

**One non-negotiable:** the score must **resolve** (not crescendo-and-stop) so that when Jay walks to the keyboard the room feels exhaled, not jolted.

## Captions and typography

- **Font:** Inter (preferred) or Söhne / GT America as alternates. Geometric sans, medium weight for body captions, semibold for the closing tagline.
- **Color palette** (match Aurora brand exactly):
  - Alpine blue: `#1e3a5f`
  - Rust: `#c2410c`
  - Sand cream: `#fef3c7`
  - Pure black: `#000000` (only used for the cut-to-black beats and final card)
- **Caption rhythm:**
  - Max **6 words on screen** at once
  - Hold for **2 seconds** minimum
  - **Fade** in/out (no hard cuts, no slide-ins, no kinetic typography)
  - Position: lower third for shots 1–4 (over the dead UI), centered for shot 5 question, lower third for shots 7–9, **centered** for the closing tagline
- **Background treatment for captions over screen capture:** subtle dark gradient (alpine-blue at 40% opacity, feathered) under text to keep contrast without boxing the type.

## Closing tagline (held for 4 seconds before cut to black)

> **Built for the worst weather. Trail-tested by experts.**

This is Aurora's existing brand line. It is the throughline the live demo will reinforce. Treatment:
- Sand cream background (`#fef3c7`), fills the frame
- Tagline centered, alpine-blue (`#1e3a5f`), Inter Semibold, ~64pt at 1080p
- No logo on this card. The tagline stands alone.
- Hold 4 seconds, then a smooth 0.5s fade to pure black before the file ends.

## Production checklist for the editor

- [ ] Stock footage and assets sourced for:
  - [ ] Generic / lifeless e-commerce UI mockups (shots 1–4). Acceptable sources: custom Figma mockups exported to MP4, or stock screen recordings from Pexels / Envato / Storyblocks. **Do not use any real retailer's UI** — risk of brand confusion or legal.
  - [ ] Patagonia-style alpine landscape b-roll (royalty-free) from Pexels or Unsplash for the homepage hero. Look for: wind-blasted ridges, alpine snow, low golden-hour light. Vertical or horizontal both fine — final hero is 16:9.
  - [ ] Screen captures of Aurora surfaces in action (shots 7–9). **Jay supplies these as 4K screen recordings** of /for-you, /concierge, /personalize, /storefront, /abandoned-cart. Editor color-grades to brand palette.
- [ ] Brand asset pack — **Jay supplies**: Aurora logo (SVG + PNG, light + dark variants), full color hex sheet, Inter font files (or confirm editor has license).
- [ ] Music license cleared via **Artlist** or **Musicbed**. Editor confirms in writing before final delivery.
- [ ] Final mix mastered at **-16 LUFS integrated**, true peak ≤ -1 dBTP. Room playback target — must not need re-mixing for a 100-person conference room.
- [ ] Captions burnt in (not as a separate sidecar — single self-contained mp4).
- [ ] Color grade: cool / desaturated for shots 1–4, warm / saturated for shots 7–10. The shift should be **felt**, not announced.
- [ ] Test playback on a laptop speaker, a conference room PA, and a single-speaker monitor. The bass thump at 1:00 should land on all three.
