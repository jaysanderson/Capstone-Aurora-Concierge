# Returns Policy

Aurora Outfitters accepts returns on most products within 90 days of purchase. This policy covers eligibility, refund processing, and exceptions.

## Standard return window

- **90 days from delivery** for product returns.
- The product must be in resale condition: unused, unwashed, with original tags, in original packaging.
- Original receipt or order confirmation email required.

## How to start a return

1. Visit your account on the website → Orders → Start a return.
2. Print the pre-paid return label.
3. Ship the product back or drop at any Aurora Outfitters service depot (28 locations across NA, EU, ANZ).
4. Refund processed within 5-7 business days of receipt.

## Refund timing

- **Credit/debit card original payment:** 5-7 business days.
- **PayPal:** 3-5 business days.
- **Store credit:** Instant on receipt processing.

Trail Club Pro members receive instant refund-to-original-payment via member-portal one-click flow — see `16-loyalty-trail-club.md`.

## Worn-in product returns (the 90-day promise)

Aurora Outfitters operates a **wear-test promise** on hiking boots and packs:

- **Aurora TerraTrek 7 (`01-product-terratrek-7.md`):** Return within 90 days even if worn, as long as the product is in functional condition and you tell us why it didn't work. We collect the feedback to inform future iterations.
- **Aurora Skyline 45L (`02-product-skyline-45l.md`):** Same 90-day wear-test return.

This is *not* an unlimited return policy. It's a return policy with the explicit honesty that boots and packs only reveal whether they fit on the trail. If they don't fit, send them back.

## What's not returnable

- **Hygiene-sensitive items** (base layers, socks) once removed from sealed packaging.
- **Items modified by the customer** (custom embroidery, dyed alterations, etc.).
- **Final-sale items** at >40% off (marked at point of purchase).
- **Repaired items** purchased second-hand through our buy-back programme.

## Refund vs. exchange

- **Refund** is the default flow.
- **Exchange** is available for size or colour swaps. The new item ships when the original arrives at our workshop; you don't have to wait for a refund to process and re-pay.

## Faulty or damaged items

Different process. **Don't start a standard return for a faulty item.**

- Email `support@outposttrails.example` with photos of the damage and your order number.
- Our support team responds within 24 hours.
- Replacement or refund is processed at no cost to you, regardless of the 90-day window.

## International returns

Aurora Outfitters ships to 47 countries. Return shipping costs are covered for:

- All US, Canada, EU, UK, Australia, New Zealand customers.
- For other countries, the customer covers return shipping; we refund the product price + original shipping.

## Returns and our sustainability commitment

Returned products are inspected. Resaleable items go back to inventory. Wear-tested items are refurbished if economical and sold through our buy-back outlet store. Items that can't be resold are recycled through our material-recovery partners. We do not landfill returned products.

This connects to our **Carbon-Negative by 2030** plan (`18-carbon-negative-2030.md`).
