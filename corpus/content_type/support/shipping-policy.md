# Shipping Policy

Aurora Outfitters ships to 47 countries.

## Standard shipping

- US: 3-5 business days, free over USD 75.
- Canada: 5-7 business days, free over USD 100.
- UK + EU: 5-10 business days, free over EUR 75.
- Australia + NZ: 5-10 business days, free over AUD 100.
- Other countries: 10-20 business days, fees apply.

## Expedited shipping

- US 2-day: USD 18
- US overnight: USD 32
- Trail Club Pro members get expedited shipping free.

## Tracking

All orders ship with tracking. Notification emails at: shipped, in transit, out for delivery, delivered.

## Carbon-negative shipping

Aurora is enrolled in carbon-negative offset programmes for all standard shipments. Carbon-Negative by 2030 commitment — see `carbon-negative-2030.md`.

## Repair-for-Life shipping

Customers shipping items for repair: pre-paid label provided through the member portal (members) or by contacting support (non-members). See `repair-for-life-programme.md`.

## Customs and import duties

International orders may incur import duties at delivery. Aurora doesn't pre-pay duties; customs declares true product value.

## Lost or damaged shipments

Email `support@aurora.example` with order number and photos. Replacement or refund within 7 days.

|---|---|---|
| Standard | Free over threshold | At cost |
| Plus | Free regardless | At cost |
