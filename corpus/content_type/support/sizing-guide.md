# Sizing Guide

This document covers Aurora Outfitters sizing for our five hero products. Each product line uses a different fit philosophy — read the section relevant to what you're buying.

## Aurora TerraTrek 7 boot — sizing

**Last:** Standard or Wide Foot.

- **Standard last** fits most feet (60-65% of customers).
- **Wide Foot last** (E width men's, equivalent for women's) fits high-volume feet, customers with bunions, customers whose feet swell on multi-day trips (almost everyone above ~50 km / week of walking sees this).

**Tips:**
- Try in the afternoon when your feet are at their largest. They'll be larger again on day 3+ of a thru-hike.
- Use the socks you'll actually walk in.
- If you're between sizes — **size up**. Toes hit the front of the boot on downhills; a small amount of room prevents black toenails.
- If your shoe is too tight after 100 km, **return for the next size** under our wear-test promise (`20-returns-policy.md`).

Full product details: `01-product-terratrek-7.md`. Mara Chen's fit notes are in `13-review-terratrek-7.md`.

## Aurora Skyline 45L pack — sizing

Aurora Skyline 45L comes in three torso sizes:

- **Short** — torso 40-46 cm (16-18 in)
- **Regular** — torso 46-52 cm (18-20.5 in)
- **Tall** — torso 52-58 cm (20.5-23 in)

**Measure your torso** — top of your hipbone to the C7 vertebra (the prominent bone at the base of your neck).

**Tips:**
- Between sizes — **size down**. The Skyline's adjustment range is wide and a smaller harness gives a cleaner load transfer.
- Hip belt sizes are integral to torso sizing — they don't swap separately.
- Trail Club Plus and Pro members get free professional pack-fitting at any service depot — see `16-loyalty-trail-club.md`.

## Aurora Helios down jacket — sizing

Standard apparel sizing — XS through XXL men's, XS through XL women's. Unisex base-layer compatible.

**Tips:**
- The Aurora Helios is designed for layering. **Size to fit over a midweight base layer comfortably.**
- It's not designed to fit under a heavy pack — don't size up to wear it under a pack hipbelt; compression destroys down loft.

## Aurora Cumulus 2P tent — capacity

Two-person spec assumes:

- Two sleeping pads up to 65 cm wide each, side-by-side.
- Backpacks stored in the vestibule, not inside the tent.
- Tall sleepers (190+ cm) may want the optional vestibule extender pole — sold separately.

## Aurora Quill 850 quilt — sizing

The Aurora Quill 850 comes in three lengths and two widths.

**Length:**
- **Short (160 cm)** — fits sleepers up to 165 cm tall.
- **Regular (180 cm)** — fits 165-185 cm.
- **Long (195 cm)** — fits 185-200 cm.

**Width:**
- **Standard** — fits sleeping pads up to 51 cm wide.
- **Wide** — fits up to 64 cm wide.

**Tips:**
- For -3 °C use, the closure straps need to hold tight to the pad — don't size up the width unnecessarily.
- The footbox closes magnetically and accommodates feet up to size 13 US.

## When in doubt

- For boots: try in store if possible. Trail Club members get same-day fittings at any service depot.
- For packs: similarly, get a professional pack-fitting. The right answer depends on torso geometry, not just height.
- For apparel: order both sizes if uncertain — our 90-day return policy (`20-returns-policy.md`) covers a single round of size swap.
- For tents: capacity is rated honestly; if you sleep large (or with a dog), consider sizing up to a 3-person tent.
