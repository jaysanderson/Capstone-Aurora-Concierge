# Trail Club — Aurora Outfitters Loyalty Programme

The Trail Club is Aurora Outfitters' loyalty programme. Three tiers. Two levers of value: free Repair-for-Life perks (see `17-repair-for-life-programme.md`) and ambassador access. Joining is free; the higher tiers require an annual purchase floor.

## The three tiers

| Tier | Annual purchase floor | Top perks |
|---|---|---|
| **Trail Club Standard** | None — anyone can join | 10% off first order; quarterly newsletter; access to seasonal product drops 24 hours early |
| **Trail Club Plus** | A$500 / year | Free first Repair-for-Life repair per year; 15% off all in-store; free pack-fitting and boot-fitting |
| **Trail Club Pro** | A$1,500 / year | Free annual workshop wash + loft restoration on down items; 20% off all in-store; priority access to limited colour drops; one ambassador-led day per year (Mara Chen / Jonah Reyes / Theo Sundberg available); 20% off Sundberg Alpine courses |

## Tier transitions

Tiers reset annually based on rolling 12-month spend. Members are upgraded automatically; downgrades take effect at year-end. Members can purchase a tier upgrade outright (Plus = A$200, Pro = A$600) if they want the perks without the spend floor.

## What "free Repair-for-Life" means at each tier

- **Standard:** No free repairs. Standard Repair-for-Life pricing applies.
- **Plus:** One free repair per year per eligible product. Excludes major reconstructions (full re-sole on the Aurora TerraTrek 7 is half-price, not free).
- **Pro:** Unlimited free first repairs (each year), plus the annual down workshop wash + loft restoration.

See `17-repair-for-life-programme.md` for the eligible-product list and what counts as a repair.

## Ambassador access (Pro only)

Trail Club Pro members can book **one ambassador-led day per year** at no additional cost. This is a guided experience, not a course. Examples:

- A day-walk on a route Mara Chen has guided 47 times (Tasmania Overland day section).
- A trail-running technique session with Jonah Reyes (when he's at home, not on a trail).
- A climbing-skills morning with Theo Sundberg in Chamonix (member arranges travel and lodging separately).

Booking opens January 1 each year via the member portal. Spots are limited.

## Sustainability connection

Trail Club tiers and the Repair-for-Life programme are connected to the **Carbon-Negative by 2030** plan (`18-carbon-negative-2030.md`). Members who participate in Repair-for-Life materially reduce the lifecycle carbon footprint of Aurora Outfitters products. Members are encouraged to repair before they replace.

## How to join

- **Standard:** Sign up via the website. Email required, nothing else.
- **Plus / Pro:** Purchase floor accumulates automatically once you've signed up Standard.
- Lifetime memberships at any tier are *not* offered. Tiers are designed to track engagement and reset annually.

## Members vs prospects in our content

A note on how our content is segmented:

- Most content (product pages, trail guides, gear reviews) is for any reader. We don't gate this.
- Member-exclusive content (booking detail for ambassador days, advanced repair workflows) is gated to Plus and Pro.
- Internal-only content (margin notes, supplier lists) is not in the public KB.
