# Aurora Outfitters 2028 Member Events

Open to all Trail Club tiers (Standard / Plus / Pro). Pro members get priority booking + complimentary admission. Plus members 50% off. Standard members full price.

## Spring 2028

**Mar 14-16 · Tasmania Overland with Mara Chen** (Cradle Mountain). Hosted in Hobart. Members + 1 guest each. AUD 480 list / AUD 240 Plus / Free Pro.

**Apr 22 · Aurora Repair-for-Life Workshop Tour** (London + Edinburgh). Hands-on tour of the Aurora workshops + Q&A with the sustainability team. Free for members.

## Summer 2028

**Jun 5-9 · Tour du Mont Blanc with Theo Sundberg** (Chamonix). 4-day guided section. Members only. EUR 1,800 list / EUR 900 Plus / EUR 540 Pro.

**Jul 19-21 · Jonah Reyes thru-hiking Q&A series** (Online). Free for all members.

## Autumn 2028

**Sep 2-4 · Patagonia W Trek planning weekend** (Online + Buenos Aires meet-up). Mara Chen leads. AUD 220 list / AUD 110 Plus / Free Pro.

**Oct 25 · Dr Anya Patel Sleep-System Science webinar** (Online). Open to all members.

## Year-round

Monthly online office hours with each ambassador. Pro members can drop in to any. Plus members can drop into one per quarter.
