# Ambassador Day Bookings — Trail Club Pro Members

Trail Club Pro members can book one ambassador-led day per year at no additional cost. This is a guided experience, not a course.

## Available ambassador days

| Ambassador | Date(s) | Location | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mara Chen | Mar-May, Sep-Nov | Tasmania Overland Track section days | 1-day guided walk |
| Mara Chen | Oct-Apr | Patagonia W Trek section | 1-day guided walk |
| Jonah Reyes | Aug-Sep (US summer only) | Sierra Nevada day routes | 1-day guided walk |
| Jonah Reyes | Nov-Mar (NZ summer) | Te Araroa Highlights section | 1-day guided walk |
| Dr Anya Patel | Year-round | Online video consultation | 30-min fit consultation |
| Theo Sundberg | Year-round | Chamonix valley climbing | 1-day climbing-skills morning |

## Booking process

1. Member opens member portal Jan 1 each year.
2. Browse calendar. Pick a slot.
3. Confirm via email within 7 days.
4. Atlas Outfitters covers ambassador time + Aurora-supplied gear.
5. Member covers their own travel and lodging.

## Cancellation policy

- 30+ days notice: free reschedule or refund (no cash; Aurora credit).
- 14-30 days: 50% credit.
- <14 days: no refund (rebook against the next year's allocation).

## Special opportunities

Trail Club Pro members who book ambassador days in slow-season windows (mid-week, off-season months) get +1 bonus day the following year.
