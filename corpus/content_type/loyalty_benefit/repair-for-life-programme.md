# Repair-for-Life — Aurora Outfitters Repair Programme

Repair-for-Life is Aurora Outfitters' commitment to keeping every eligible product in the field for as long as the customer wants it. We repair, we re-sole, we re-down. We don't replace as a first step. Most products are eligible; pricing varies by repair type and member tier.

## What "for life" means

We commit to repairing eligible products for as long as you own them, subject to component availability. We hold replacement components for in-production models for the full retail life of the model plus 7 years. After retirement, we hold components for the longest of: 7 more years OR until we run out (whichever is later).

## Eligible products

- **Aurora TerraTrek 7 (and all TerraTrek models from v4 onwards)** — resoles, eyelet replacement, leather re-stitching, GripCore re-bond.
- **Aurora Skyline 45L (and all Skyline models from 2022 onwards)** — hip belt swap, frame stay replacement, shoulder strap re-stitch.
- **Aurora Helios down jacket** — workshop wash + loft restoration, seam re-tape, zip replacement.
- **Aurora Cumulus 2P tent** — pole replacement, fly seam re-tape, mesh patch, stake-loop reinforcement.
- **Aurora Quill 850 quilt** — workshop wash + loft restoration, baffle re-fill, magnetic snap replacement.

Products from other brands are not eligible. Custom modifications void Repair-for-Life eligibility on the affected component.

## What counts as a "repair" vs a "reconstruction"

Repairs are individual component replacements or restorations. Reconstructions are major rebuilds. Pricing differs.

- **Repairs (e.g., Aurora Helios seam re-tape):** A$25-150. Free for Trail Club Plus and Pro members.
- **Reconstructions (e.g., Aurora TerraTrek 7 full resole + welt reset):** A$80-200. 50% off for Trail Club Plus members; free for Trail Club Pro (one per product per year cap).

See member tier details in `16-loyalty-trail-club.md`.

## How to submit a repair

1. Bring the product to any Aurora Outfitters service depot (28 locations across NA, EU, ANZ).
2. Or post to the central Adelaide workshop with the pre-paid label from the member portal.
3. Workshop receives → assessment → quote → repair → ship back.
4. Standard turnaround: 14 calendar days domestic, 21 international. Rush turnaround (7 days) available for Trail Club Pro members.

## What we won't do

- Resole boots that have walked over 5,000 km in their current shoe geometry (the upper has fatigued past sole replacement value).
- Patch tents that have experienced UV degradation beyond a defined threshold (we measure tear strength).
- Reload down items that have been previously washed with detergent (the down has been chemically altered).

These limits exist because we won't ship back something that won't perform. We'll always tell you why.

## The carbon argument

Repair-for-Life is the lever that lets us hit our **Carbon-Negative by 2030** target (`18-carbon-negative-2030.md`). A repaired product carries about 8% of the embodied carbon of a new product. A typical Aurora Outfitters customer who participates in Repair-for-Life keeps their gear in service 2.4x longer than the industry average, and that's the math we need.

## Member benefits summary

- **Standard tier:** Pay per repair at the rates above.
- **Plus tier:** One free repair per eligible product per year. 50% off reconstructions.
- **Pro tier:** Unlimited free first repairs. Free annual workshop wash + loft restoration on down items. One free reconstruction per product per year.
