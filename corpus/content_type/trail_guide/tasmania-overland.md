# Trail Guide — Tasmania Overland Track

The Overland Track is Australia's premier multi-day alpine traverse. 65 km point-to-point through the World Heritage Cradle Mountain–Lake St Clair National Park, traditionally walked over six days. Booked in advance October through May; walkable but unbooked outside the booking season with appropriate experience. This guide is for the standard six-day October-May itinerary in either direction.

## Quick facts

- **Distance:** 65 km point-to-point
- **Days:** 6 (standard) — 4 (fast) — 8 (with side trips)
- **Elevation gain:** ~2,100 m cumulative
- **Best season:** Oct - May (booked); experienced walkers Jun-Sep
- **Sleeping options:** Public huts (first-come) + tent platforms
- **Permits:** Booked through the Tasmania Parks and Wildlife Service

## Day-by-day

**Day 1 — Ronny Creek to Waterfall Valley (10 km).** Cradle Mountain summit detour optional (3-4 hours return). Hut sleeps 24.

**Day 2 — Waterfall Valley to Lake Windermere (8 km).** Short day. Side trip to Lake Will recommended.

**Day 3 — Lake Windermere to Pelion Hut (16 km).** Longest day. Mount Pelion East side trip if weather holds.

**Day 4 — Pelion Hut to Kia Ora (8 km).** Mount Ossa (Tasmania's highest peak, 1,617 m) side trip — 4-6 hours return.

**Day 5 — Kia Ora to Windy Ridge (10 km).** Three waterfall side trips available.

**Day 6 — Windy Ridge to Narcissus Hut, ferry across Lake St Clair (10 km + ferry).** Pre-book the Narcissus-Cynthia Bay ferry.

## Gear recommendations

Mara Chen has guided the Overland Track 47 times. Her packing recommendations:

- **Boots:** Aurora TerraTrek 7 — see `01-product-terratrek-7.md`. Pre-broken in. Wide Foot fit if your feet swell on day 3+.
- **Pack:** Aurora Skyline 45L — see `02-product-skyline-45l.md`. The brain pocket holds your hut docket cleanly.
- **Tent:** Aurora Cumulus 2P — see `04-product-cumulus-2p-tent.md`. The huts can be full in peak season.
- **Insulation:** Aurora Helios down jacket — see `03-product-helios-jacket.md`. Evening temperatures drop to 2-5 °C even in summer.
- **Sleep:** Aurora Quill 850 quilt — see `05-product-quill-850-quilt.md`.

## Safety notes

Hypothermia is the genuine risk in any season. Tasmania weather changes inside an hour. See `22-altitude-acclimatisation.md` for sub-2,000-m exertion considerations (you'll be moving fast at moderate elevation; pace matters).
