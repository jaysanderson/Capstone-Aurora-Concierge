# Trail Guide — Laugavegur (Iceland)

Iceland's most-walked multi-day trail. 55 km point-to-point through volcanic terrain, hot springs, and glacier-fed rivers. Walked in 3-5 days during the short Icelandic walking season (mid-June through early September).

## Quick facts

- **Distance:** 55 km point-to-point
- **Days:** 4 standard
- **Best season:** Mid-Jun to early-Sep (huts open)
- **Sleeping:** Mountain huts (Ferðafélag Íslands) — book months ahead
- **Tent option:** Allowed at hut campsites; can be exposed in storms
- **River crossings:** Multiple; some require wading (cold water)

## Day-by-day

**Day 1 — Landmannalaugar to Hrafntinnusker** (12 km, +470 m) — Hot springs at start.
**Day 2 — Hrafntinnusker to Álftavatn** (12 km, -490 m) — Glacier-fed lake.
**Day 3 — Álftavatn to Emstrur** (15 km, gentle) — River crossings.
**Day 4 — Emstrur to Þórsmörk** (15 km, -300 m) — Finish in forested valley.

## Weather warnings

Iceland summer weather: 5-20 °C with regular storms. Hypothermia is a real risk. Hardshell + Aurora Helios + waterproof boots are not optional.
