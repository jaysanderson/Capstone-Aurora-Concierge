# Trail Guide — Patagonia W Trek (Torres del Paine)

The W Trek is the marquee multi-day route in Chilean Patagonia's Torres del Paine National Park. 80 km in a W-shaped loop visiting Glacier Grey, the Frances Valley, and the Towers themselves. Five days standard, four days fast, six days unhurried. The weather is the headline.

## Quick facts

- **Distance:** ~80 km
- **Days:** 5 (standard) — 4 (fast) — 6 (unhurried)
- **Elevation gain:** ~3,400 m cumulative
- **Best season:** Oct - Apr (Southern Hemisphere summer)
- **Sleeping options:** Refugios (book ahead) or camping (book ahead)
- **Permits:** Park entry + accommodation booking via Vertice / Las Torres

## The weather

Patagonia in season is famously volatile. Wind speeds over 60 km/h are routine in the open valleys. November and March-April are statistically the best windows. December and January are wind-peak months with the longest daylight (15+ hours).

## Day-by-day (west-to-east)

**Day 1 — Refugio Paine Grande to Refugio Grey (11 km).** First view of Glacier Grey. Mostly flat.

**Day 2 — Glacier Grey day and return to Paine Grande (15 km).** Walk to the upper Mirador for an aerial view of the glacier.

**Day 3 — Paine Grande to Refugio Frances or Cuernos (15 km).** Lake Skottsberg overlook.

**Day 4 — Frances Valley up-and-down (16 km).** The British Mirador is the day's high point.

**Day 5 — Cuernos to Hotel Las Torres + Torres day hike (22 km).** The Towers themselves — start in the dark for sunrise from the Mirador.

## Gear recommendations

Mara Chen has run Patagonia trips for Aurora Outfitters since 2018.

- **Insulation:** Aurora Helios down jacket is mandatory — see `03-product-helios-jacket.md`. Wind chill on the Towers can drop apparent temperature below -5 °C in summer.
- **Hardshell:** Required. Rain falls horizontally in 60 km/h wind.
- **Boots:** Aurora TerraTrek 7 — see `01-product-terratrek-7.md`. Patagonian trails are mixed rock and bog; ankle support helps.
- **Pack:** Aurora Skyline 45L — see `02-product-skyline-45l.md`. Five-day food carry is feasible if you book refugio meals on at least one night.
