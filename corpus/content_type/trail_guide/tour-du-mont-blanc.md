# Trail Guide — Tour du Mont Blanc (TMB)

The Tour du Mont Blanc is Europe's most-walked multi-day trail. 170 km circular route through France, Italy, and Switzerland encircling the Mont Blanc massif. Walked in 7-11 days depending on pace and how many side trips you take. Hut-to-hut available the entire route; camping is feasible but restricted in places.

## Quick facts

- **Distance:** ~170 km
- **Days:** 7 (fast) — 10 (standard) — 11+ (with side trips)
- **Elevation gain:** ~10,000 m cumulative
- **Best season:** Mid-Jun to mid-Sep
- **Sleeping:** Mountain refuges (book months ahead) + selected camping areas
- **Direction:** Counter-clockwise from Les Houches is traditional

## Sample standard itinerary

**Day 1 — Les Houches to Les Contamines (16 km).** Easy first day to acclimatise.

**Day 2 — Les Contamines to Refuge du Bonhomme (15 km).** First major climb.

**Day 3 — Bonhomme to Refuge des Mottets (11 km).** Border into Italy.

**Day 4 — Mottets to Courmayeur (17 km).** Long descent into Aosta Valley.

**Day 5 — Courmayeur to Refuge Bonatti (18 km).** Most famous viewpoint of the trip.

**Day 6 — Bonatti to La Fouly (Switzerland) (16 km).** Into Switzerland.

**Day 7 — La Fouly to Champex (14 km).** Lakeside village.

**Day 8 — Champex to Trient (15 km).** Cross back into France via Col de Balme.

**Day 9 — Trient to Tre-le-Champ (10 km).** Short day.

**Day 10 — Tre-le-Champ to Les Houches (18 km).** Final ridge above Chamonix.

## Gear recommendations

Theo Sundberg (climbing instructor, 22 years in the Alps) on TMB gear:

- **Boots:** Aurora TerraTrek 7 — see `01-product-terratrek-7.md`. Trail conditions vary from cobbled village paths to scree, daily.
- **Pack:** Aurora Skyline 45L — see `02-product-skyline-45l.md`. Mountain-hut sleeping means less personal gear; 45 L is enough.
- **Insulation:** Aurora Helios down jacket — see `03-product-helios-jacket.md`. Refuges can be cold at altitude.

## Weather notes

Afternoon thunderstorms are common above 2,000 m in July and August. Start early; aim to be off exposed ridges by 13:00. See `22-altitude-acclimatisation.md` for thinking about exertion at 2,000-2,500 m.

## Booking

Refuges fill in February for the following summer. Tour operators (UTMB, KE Adventure, Macs Adventure) pre-book and trek-support if you don't want the logistics.
