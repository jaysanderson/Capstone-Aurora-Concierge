# Trail Guide — Yosemite High Sierra Loop

The High Sierra Loop is a classic 7-day backpacking circuit through Yosemite National Park visiting the High Sierra Camps (when operational). 60 miles, ~10,000 ft of elevation gain.

## Quick facts

- **Distance:** ~60 mi (97 km)
- **Days:** 6-8 (standard 7)
- **Elevation gain:** ~3,000 m cumulative
- **Best season:** Jul-Sep (snow-dependent)
- **Permits:** Wilderness permits required (lottery)
- **Sleeping:** Backcountry camping or High Sierra Camps (separate booking, when operational)

## Day-by-day

**Day 1 — Tuolumne Meadows to Glen Aulin** (6 mi, +200 ft)
**Day 2 — Glen Aulin to May Lake** (10 mi, +1,800 ft)
**Day 3 — May Lake to Sunrise** (8 mi, +1,400 ft)
**Day 4 — Sunrise to Merced Lake** (12 mi, -2,400 ft)
**Day 5 — Merced Lake to Vogelsang** (8 mi, +2,100 ft)
**Day 6 — Vogelsang to Tuolumne Meadows** (8 mi, -1,500 ft)

## Gear recommendations

Jonah Reyes guided his first long-trail trip on this loop in 2018.

- **Boots:** Aurora TerraTrek 7 — see `terratrek-7.md`. Granite slabs and forest singletrack alternate.
- **Pack:** Aurora Skyline 45L — see `skyline-45l.md`. Bear canister mandatory; fits horizontally.
- **Tent:** Aurora Cumulus 2P if sleeping in High Sierra Camps backup — see `cumulus-2p-tent.md`.
- **Quilt:** Aurora Quill 850 — see `quill-850-quilt.md`. -3 °C comfort handles August nights.

## Weather and altitude

Peak elevation ~3,200 m. Most walkers don't experience altitude issues at this height but caution applies — see `altitude-acclimatisation.md`.

## Permits

Wilderness permits via recreation.gov lottery. High Sierra Camps booking when operational opens January each year and books out within hours.

## Why this trek matters

Yosemite is the entry point for many NORAM customers' multi-day backpacking. The High Sierra Loop is the natural progression after first single-night trips.
