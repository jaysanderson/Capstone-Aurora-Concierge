# Trail Guide — Te Araroa Highlights (New Zealand)

Te Araroa is New Zealand's 3,000 km north-south thru-trail from Cape Reinga to Bluff. This guide covers the recommended 100-200 km section-hike highlights for first-time visitors.

## The trail

The full Te Araroa is typically walked over 4-5 months. The highlights itinerary picks the best South Island sections: Queen Charlotte Track + Nelson Lakes + Routeburn.

## Section: Queen Charlotte Track (3 days, 71 km)

- Sounds-edge walking. Water-taxi resupply.
- Sleeps: hostels or campsites.
- Boots: Aurora TerraTrek 7 — see `terratrek-7.md`.

## Section: Nelson Lakes (4 days, 80 km)

- Lake circuits. Hut-based.
- Sleeps: DOC huts (book ahead).
- Insulation: Aurora Helios — see `helios-jacket.md`. Cold alpine nights.

## Section: Routeburn (3 days, 32 km, separate booking)

- Routeburn Great Walk requires separate booking through DOC.
- Aurora Skyline 45L pack — see `skyline-45l.md`.
