# Carbon-Negative by 2030 — Aurora Outfitters' Climate Commitment

Aurora Outfitters has committed to becoming carbon-negative across our full Scope-1, Scope-2, and Scope-3 emissions by 2030. This document is our public plan, our progress to date, and our remaining work. It is updated annually.

## Definitions

- **Scope 1:** Direct emissions from operations we control (e.g., our workshops, our vehicles, our owned offices).
- **Scope 2:** Indirect emissions from purchased energy (e.g., electricity for our retail locations).
- **Scope 3:** All other emissions including our supply chain, the embodied carbon in our products, shipping to customers, and the use-phase impact of our products in the field.

Scope 3 is the largest and hardest. It represents roughly 87% of our total emissions for a typical retail-fashion business; Aurora Outfitters' Scope 3 is around 82% because of our repair focus.

## The plan

**By 2025 (achieved):**
- Operations powered 100% by renewable energy (Scope 2 → 0).
- All retail locations on green electricity.
- Carbon accounting per-product live in the back office (each SKU has an embodied-carbon line item).

**By 2027 (in progress, ~60% achieved):**
- Supply chain carbon disclosure from all tier-1 suppliers.
- Repair-for-Life delivers measurable carbon reduction at portfolio level — see `17-repair-for-life-programme.md`.
- 80% of products use recycled or biobased fabrics by weight.

**By 2030 (target):**
- Net negative across all scopes.
- Carbon-removal partnerships fund the negative balance.
- Per-product carbon labelling visible to customers at point of sale.

A typical multi-day backpacker buys ~22 kg CO2e of new gear in their first year. Our average customer's gear-replacement footprint over 10 years is ~37 kg CO2e because of Repair-for-Life. Industry average over 10 years is ~89 kg CO2e.

## What customers can do

- **Participate in Repair-for-Life.** Repair before replace. The single highest-impact thing an individual customer can do.
- **Choose recycled-content products.** We label these explicitly.
- **Don't over-buy.** A long-mile thru-hiker doesn't need a different pack for the AT and the PCT.
- **Resell or donate.** Aurora Outfitters operates a buy-back programme for select used products in Trail Club Plus / Pro tiers.

## What we won't do

- Buy offsets that don't have third-party verification.
- Make claims we can't measure.
- Greenwash the Aurora Helios's down sourcing — we use RDS-certified down because we believe it; we don't claim it's "carbon-neutral down" because that's misleading.
