# Built for the Worst Weather

One of Aurora Outfitters' four brand pillars (alongside Trail-Tested by Experts, Repairable for Life, and Carbon-Negative by 2030).

## What it means

Every Aurora product is spec'd against the conditions the worst customer journey will throw at it. Not the marketing photos. The reality.

## How we apply it

- **Aurora TerraTrek 7** — tested against Tasmania mud + Mont Blanc cobbles + Patagonia volcanic scree. Mara Chen carries the field data.
- **Aurora Helios** — tested against Patagonian wind chill + Iceland horizontal rain + Annapurna pre-dawn cold. See temperature rating analysis in `helios-rating.md`.
- **Aurora Cumulus 2P** — tested against 50 km/h wind events in the Pyrenees. See `cumulus-2p-review.md`.
- **Aurora Skyline 45L** — tested against thru-hiker sustained-load wear over 4,800+ km on Jonah Reyes' Triple Crown.

## What we won't do

- Specify a product against best-case conditions only.
- Marketing photos that imply weather conditions we haven't tested in.
- Discount actual performance for the sake of weight savings.

## Field-test cycle

Every new Aurora product undergoes minimum 1 full field season with at least 2 ambassadors before going to GA. We've delayed launches when the field data didn't support the specs.

## Customer benefit

Aurora customers buying for the worst-case trip can trust the spec. That's the entire promise.
