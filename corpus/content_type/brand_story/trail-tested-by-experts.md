# Trail-Tested by Experts

Every product Aurora Outfitters ships has been field-tested by named ambassadors with hundreds of trail-miles in the product's category before GA.

## Our ambassadors

- **Mara Chen** — alpine guide, 15 years. Tasmania (47 Overland Track guides), Patagonia, Greater Patagonian Trail.
- **Jonah Reyes** — Triple Crown thru-hiker. PCT, AT, CDT.
- **Dr Anya Patel** — sports physiologist. Lab data + 30-min fit consultations.
- **Theo Sundberg** — UIAGM/IFMGA climbing instructor, 22 years. Chamonix-based.

Each ambassador owns one or more product lines:
- TerraTrek + Helios + Quill: Mara
- Skyline + Quill: Jonah
- Cumulus + Crag XR: Theo
- Fit science: Anya

## Why this matters

Most outdoor brands pay influencers to promote products. Aurora pays ambassadors to *change* the product. Mara's input drove the Wide Foot TerraTrek 7 last. Jonah's data drove the Skyline 45L hip-belt foam choice. Anya's data drove the Quill 850 width sizing decisions.

## What we'll do

- Name every ambassador on the relevant product page.
- Publish ambassador-authored content (gear reviews, trail guides, podcasts).
- Let ambassadors say "this isn't right for that trip" on the record (see Mara's review in `terratrek-7.md`).

## What we won't do

- Use unnamed influencers.
- Buy reviews.
- Suppress critical ambassador feedback.

## Customer benefit

When an Aurora product is recommended for an activity or destination, it's because a named ambassador with verifiable experience said so on the record.
