# Aurora Quill 850 — Ultralight Down Quilt

The Quill 850 is Aurora Outfitters' ultralight down quilt — a top quilt designed to replace a traditional mummy bag for warm sleepers on multi-day trips. 850-fill responsibly-sourced down, 10 D shell, draft collar, footbox closure with magnetic snap.

## Specs

- **Fill:** 280 g of 850-fill responsibly-sourced down
- **Shell:** 10 D nylon with PFC-free DWR
- **Comfort rating:** -3 °C (sleeper-dependent)
- **Limit rating:** -8 °C
- **Weight:** 540 g (Regular)
- **Lengths:** Short (160 cm), Regular (180 cm), Long (195 cm)
- **Widths:** Standard, Wide

Jonah Reyes' base setup on the Triple Crown:

## Recommended for

- Warm sleepers on multi-day trips down to -3 °C nominal.
- Activities: Thru-Hiking, Day-Hiking overnighter, Bikepacking, summer Alpine.
- Destinations: Tasmania Overland Track, Tour du Mont Blanc, Annapurna Circuit (below 4,200 m).
