# Aurora Crag XR — Technical Climbing Harness

The Aurora Crag XR is our flagship climbing harness. Designed with Theo Sundberg (climbing instructor, 22 years in Chamonix), this is the Trail-Tested by Experts category leader. **Exclusive to Trail Club Plus members and above.**

## Specs

- **Weight:** 380 g (Medium)
- **Sizes:** XS, S, M, L, XL
- **Belay loops:** Single, double-stitched, 16 kN rated
- **Gear loops:** 4 (two front-mounted, two rear-mounted)
- **Adjustment:** Dual drop-seat with quick-release buckles
- **Repair-for-Life eligible**

## What ambassadors say

Theo Sundberg uses the Crag XR on every alpine course he teaches:

> "The Crag XR is what I take into Chamonix every day. The drop-seat buckles are field-serviceable; the belay loop reinforcement is overbuilt where it needs to be. Two hours of multi-pitch on the Aiguilles and you forget you're wearing it."

## Recommended for

- Multi-pitch sport and alpine climbing.
- Day-long hanging belays.
- Activities: Alpine Climbing, Multi-Pitch Sport.
- Destinations: Tour du Mont Blanc (technical sections), Yosemite High Sierra.

## Why it's Trail Club only

Climbing gear sells with much higher liability than soft goods. Trail Club members complete a one-time safety acknowledgement at purchase, which keeps the climbing range available to all members at competitive price without driving non-climber margin questions across the rest of the catalog.
