# Aurora Cumulus 2P — Two-Person Backpacking Tent

The Cumulus 2P is a two-person, three-season backpacking tent designed for shared multi-day carries on the Tasmania Overland Track, the Tour du Mont Blanc, and similar trails. Single-vestibule freestanding pole architecture, 1.2 kg packed weight, full mesh inner for warm-night ventilation.

## Specs

- **Capacity:** 2 person with two 65 cm sleeping pads side-by-side
- **Packed weight:** 1.20 kg (poles, fly, inner)
- **Floor area:** 2.6 m²
- **Peak height:** 102 cm
- **Vestibule:** Single asymmetric vestibule, 0.8 m²
- **Pole material:** DAC Featherlite NSL aluminium
- **Fly:** 15D ripstop, 1200 mm hydrostatic head, PFC-free DWR
- **Repair-for-Life eligible** — see `17-repair-for-life-programme.md`
