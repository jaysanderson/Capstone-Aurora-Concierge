# Aurora TerraTrek 7 — All-Terrain Hiking Boot

The TerraTrek 7 is Aurora Outfitters' flagship four-season hiking boot. Designed with alpine guide Mara Chen across three field seasons in Tasmania, Patagonia, and the Tour du Mont Blanc, the Aurora TerraTrek 7 trades the weight of a full mountaineering boot for the long-mile comfort of a trail runner. Waterproof to 200 mm sustained immersion, full-grain leather upper, dual-density EVA midsole, and our proprietary GripCore outsole.

## Specs

- **Weight (per boot, size 9 US M):** 580 g
- **Sizes:** US M 7-14 (half sizes 8-12); US W 5.5-11 (half sizes 6-10)
- **Width:** Standard and **Wide Foot** profiles available — see `21-sizing-guide.md`
- **Waterproofing:** PFC-free DWR + sealed bootie construction
- **Repair-for-Life eligible** — see `17-repair-for-life-programme.md`

## Recommended for

- Day Hiking and Thru-Hiking up to ~30 kg pack weight.
- Activities: Day Hiking, Thru-Hiking, Light Alpine Climbing.
- Destinations: Tasmania Overland Track, Tour du Mont Blanc, Annapurna Circuit.
