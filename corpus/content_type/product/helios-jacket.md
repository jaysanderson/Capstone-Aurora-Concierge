# Aurora Helios Down Jacket — 850-Fill Mountain Insulation

The Helios is Aurora Outfitters' premium down-fill jacket. 850-fill-power responsibly-sourced down (RDS-certified) inside a 10D ripstop shell with PFC-free DWR. Designed for static warmth in alpine starts, basecamp evenings, and shoulder-season layering. Recommended down to -15 °C with a midweight base layer.

## Specs

- **Fill:** 130 g of 850-fill responsibly-sourced down (RDS certified)
- **Shell:** 10 denier ripstop nylon, PFC-free DWR
- **Weight:** 380 g (Men's M)
- **Packed size:** 8 cm × 16 cm (compresses into its own chest pocket)
- **Sizes:** XS-XXL men's; XS-XL women's; unisex base layer compatible
- **Repair-for-Life eligible** — see `17-repair-for-life-programme.md`

## Recommended for

- Static warmth in cold camps, summit pushes, basecamp evenings.
- Activities: Day Hiking, Alpine Climbing, Thru-Hiking shoulder season.
- Destinations: Patagonia W Trek (Oct-Apr), Annapurna Circuit, Tour du Mont Blanc.

## Layering

- Wear over a midweight base layer for active warmth down to -5 °C.
- Wear under a hardshell for static warmth down to -15 °C.
- Don't wear under a heavy pack — compression kills loft over time.
