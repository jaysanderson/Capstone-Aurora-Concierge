# Aurora Stratus 1P — Solo Backpacking Tent (preview)

**Status:** Pre-launch (Spring 2029). Trail Club Pro members get first-look access Q4 2028.

The Aurora Stratus 1P is our first solo-traveller-focused tent. Designed for Pacific Crest Trail-style sub-1 kg base weights. Single-pole, single-door, asymmetric pitch. Built on the same Repairable for Life and Carbon-Negative by 2030 commitments as the rest of the Aurora range.

## Specs (target)

- **Packed weight:** 920 g
- **Floor area:** 2.0 m²
- **Peak height:** 92 cm
- **Pole:** DAC Featherlite NSL aluminium
- **Fly:** 10D ripstop with PFC-free DWR
- **Repair-for-Life eligible**

## Designed with

Jonah Reyes contributed iterative feedback across the 2027 PCT season. Field tested on 3 weeks of Sierra Nevada solo travel.

## Recommended for

- Solo travellers on multi-day routes.
- Activities: Thru-Hiking, Day Hiking overnighter, Bikepacking.
- Destinations: Tasmania Overland Track (solo), Annapurna Circuit lower section, all NORAM thru-trails.

- Aurora Quill 850 — see `quill-850-quilt.md`.

## Member preview

Trail Club Pro members can request a Stratus 1P preview pair through the member portal Q4 2028. Limited to 200 units.

## Sustainability

100% recycled fly fabric. Aluminium pole recyclable at end-of-life.
