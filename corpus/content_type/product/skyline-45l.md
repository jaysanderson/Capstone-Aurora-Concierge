# Aurora Skyline 45L — Multi-Day Trekking Pack

The Skyline 45L is built for week-long fastpacking and mid-weight thru-hiking. 45 litres of total volume across one main compartment, a brain pocket, and twin hip-belt pockets. Designed against ambassador Jonah Reyes' brief — he carried prototype Skyline packs through the Triple Crown.

## Specs

- **Volume:** 45 L (main 38 L + brain 4 L + hip pockets 3 L)
- **Weight:** 920 g (size Regular)
- **Frame:** Aluminium stays + foam-back panel, supports up to 13 kg comfortably
- **Sizes:** Short (torso 40-46 cm), Regular (46-52 cm), Tall (52-58 cm)
- **Repair-for-Life eligible** — see `17-repair-for-life-programme.md`

## What ambassadors say

Jonah Reyes (Triple Crown thru-hiker, 4,800+ trail miles):

> "I tested four prototype iterations of the Aurora Skyline 45L on the Continental Divide Trail in 2024. The hip belt's V-shape took 200 km to settle into the foam, then disappeared. For seven-day food carries, this is what I reach for."

Full review in `14-review-skyline-45l.md`.

## Recommended for

- Multi-day backpacking from 3 to 10 nights.
- Thru-hiker resupply legs — fits standard bear canisters horizontally.
- Activities: Thru-Hiking, Alpine Climbing (under 20 kg loads), Bikepacking.
- Destinations: Annapurna Circuit, Patagonia W Trek, Tour du Mont Blanc, Tasmania Overland Track.

## Sizing

If you're between sizes, **size down** — the Aurora Skyline 45L's adjustment range is wide and a smaller harness gives a cleaner load transfer. See `21-sizing-guide.md`.
