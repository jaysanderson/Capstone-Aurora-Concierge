# Gear Review — Aurora Quill 850 (by Jonah Reyes)

**Reviewer:** Jonah Reyes, Triple Crown thru-hiker
**Test:** 4,800+ km across CDT + PCT + selected Te Araroa sections

## The headline

The Aurora Quill 850 has replaced my mummy bag for everything except deep winter expeditions. For shoulder-season thru-hiking, this is the warmest 540 g of down you can buy.

## What works

**The magnetic footbox.** Cowboy-camping mornings are 10 seconds of unsnap rather than 90 seconds of zipper dance.
**The 850-fill loft.** Holds spec over many washes if you let Aurora do the workshop wash.
**The 10D shell.** Survived four years of beta testing including some unintentional abuse.

## What's less good

**Width sizing.** I'm a side-sleeper and the Standard width feels constrained. Order Wide if you sleep on your side.
**The colour.** Olive looks great in the marketing photos and shows every stain in real life.

## Pairs with

- Aurora Helios as a head-piece + extra layer — see `helios-jacket.md`.
- Aurora Cumulus 2P tent — see `cumulus-2p-tent.md`.

## Repair-for-Life

Workshop wash + loft restoration. Trail Club Pro members get one free per year. This is the best Aurora benefit.
