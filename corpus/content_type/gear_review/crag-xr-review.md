# Gear Review — Aurora Crag XR (by Theo Sundberg)

**Reviewer:** Theo Sundberg, IFMGA-certified guide
**Test:** Daily use in Chamonix courses, 2026-2028

## The headline

The Aurora Crag XR is what I take into Chamonix every day. The drop-seat buckles are field-serviceable. The belay loop reinforcement is overbuilt where it needs to be. Two hours of multi-pitch on the Aiguilles and you forget you're wearing it.

## What works

**Drop-seat buckles.** Field-serviceable. Tested with clients who needed adjustment mid-route.
**Single 16 kN belay loop.** Conservative spec. Inspires confidence in apprentice guides.
**Four gear loops in good positions.** Two front, two rear. Sport, trad, or alpine layouts all work.

## What's less good

**Trail Club Plus exclusivity.** I get the liability rationale but it means non-member climbers can't buy our best harness. We should reconsider.
**Sizing curve.** XS runs small for women with smaller hip circumference. Try in person.

## Pairs with

- Aurora Helios for cold belays — see `helios-jacket.md`.
- Aurora Cumulus 2P for multi-day approaches — see `cumulus-2p-tent.md`.

## Repair-for-Life

Buckle replacement covered. Webbing inspection annual (recommended, free at service depot).

## Why this matters

Liability gear sells with safety acknowledgements. The Trail Club Plus model makes the safety acknowledgement an upfront customer commitment. It's the right tradeoff for climbing gear.
