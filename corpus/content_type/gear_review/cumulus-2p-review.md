# Gear Review — Aurora Cumulus 2P (by Theo Sundberg)

**Reviewer:** Theo Sundberg, climbing instructor, 22 years
**Test:** 60+ pitches in the Cumulus 2P across the Alps, Pyrenees, and Dolomites

## The headline

The Aurora Cumulus 2P is the right tent for shared multi-day backpacking in three-season conditions. It's not the lightest 2P tent (cottage-industry sub-1 kg exists). It's the lightest 2P tent with real wind resistance and pole architecture you can patch in the field.

## What works

**The DAC Featherlite poles.** Tested in 50 km/h wind events in the Pyrenees. Held.
**The asymmetric vestibule.** Fits a 75 L pack flat.
**The fly seam tape.** Five years of UV exposure later still sheds.

## What's less good

**Single vestibule.** Two-person partners will want two doors. Add the Cumulus 2P Plus pole kit for a second.
**Stake bag.** Always undersized. Buy more stakes.

## Pairs with

- Aurora Skyline 45L — see `skyline-45l.md`.

## Who shouldn't buy

- Solo travellers (consider Aurora Stratus 1P when available).
- Four-season expeditioners (need a true 4-season tent).

## Repair-for-Life

Pole replacement covered. Fly re-tape covered. Stake replacement free at any service depot.
