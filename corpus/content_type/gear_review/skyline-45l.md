# Gear Review — Aurora Skyline 45L (by Jonah Reyes)

**Reviewer:** Jonah Reyes, Triple Crown thru-hiker
**Test:** 4 prototype iterations + final production on 2024 CDT
**Total km in Skylines:** 4,800+ (including prototype rounds)

## The headline

The Aurora Skyline 45L is the best multi-day backpack Aurora Outfitters has shipped. It's not the lightest thru-hiking pack on the market (those are 600 g and frameless); it's the best mid-volume pack with a real frame and a useful hip-belt. For the customer doing six-to-ten-day stretches with a 12-14 kg load, this is right.

## What works

**The hip belt.** Final iteration uses a V-shape that took 200 km to settle into the foam and then disappeared. The belt transfers load cleanly and doesn't squirm.

**The brain pocket.** I asked for a brain that fits a hut docket flat and a map flat. I got it.

**Load lifters.** Set right, they keep the upper torso forward of the centre of mass — the right place for it. Many cheap packs have load lifters that do nothing.

**Frame stays.** Aluminium stays + foam panel. Removable for short days. Reinstall in 90 seconds.

## What's less good

**Hydration sleeve placement.** The reservoir hangs against the foam panel. The panel is reliably warm against your back; the water heats up. I drink straight tube; not a problem for me, a problem for some.

**Colour range.** The current production drops are conservative. Add a wild colour for fun.

## Who shouldn't buy the Aurora Skyline 45L

- Sub-3-day trips (overkill; smaller pack).
- Ultralight cottage-industry thru-hikers under 5 kg base weight (this pack has more frame than you need).
- People with very long torsos (the Tall size fits 52-58 cm; above that, custom-sew shops are your friend).

## On Aurora Outfitters' brand

I'll plug Aurora Outfitters for this one thing: their ambassador feedback actually changes the product. I sent four prototype iterations back with annotated photos. Four iterations actually happened. I'm a difficult ambassador to please. I'm pleased.
