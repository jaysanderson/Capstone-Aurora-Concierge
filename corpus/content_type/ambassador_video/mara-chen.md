# Mara Chen — Alpine Guide & Aurora Outfitters Ambassador

Mara Chen has been an alpine guide for 15 years, with seasons in the Cascades, Patagonia, the Greater Patagonian Trail, Tasmania, and the European Alps. She joined Aurora Outfitters as a product ambassador in 2019 and now collaborates on the TerraTrek, Aurora Helios, and Quill ranges.

## Background

Mara grew up in Vancouver, started climbing at 11, qualified as an IFMGA-certified guide at 27 after seasons in Squamish and the Coast Mountains. Her commercial guiding focuses on multi-week expedition support — Patagonian fjord traverses, Tasmania crossings, and the Greater Patagonian Trail. She has personally walked the Tasmania Overland Track 47 times.

## What Mara works on with Aurora Outfitters

- **Aurora Helios down jacket:** Mara was the field tester for three prototype Aurora Helios iterations. Her data drove the choice to move from a 14 D shell to a 10 D shell in the final design — the weight saving (45 g) was worth the small durability trade.
- **Aurora TerraTrek 7:** Her input shaped the wide-foot fit option. Her observation was that long-mile guides see foot swelling on day 3 onwards, and the standard last didn't accommodate that.
- **Aurora Quill 850:** Mara's testing on shoulder-season Patagonia trips confirmed the -3 °C comfort rating with a midweight base layer.

## Mara on why she works with Aurora Outfitters

> "I came to Aurora Outfitters because I was tired of being asked to plug gear I didn't use. The team here lets me say 'this isn't right for that trip' on the record. The Aurora TerraTrek 7 wasn't right for technical alpine routes; we put that in the marketing. The Aurora Helios isn't right under a heavy pack; we put that in the marketing. That's why I'm still here."
