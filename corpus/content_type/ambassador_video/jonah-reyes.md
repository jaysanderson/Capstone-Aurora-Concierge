# Jonah Reyes — Triple Crown Thru-Hiker

Jonah Reyes is one of fewer than 600 hikers who have completed the Triple Crown of US long-distance trails: the Pacific Crest Trail (4,265 km), the Appalachian Trail (3,524 km), and the Continental Divide Trail (5,000 km). Across those three trails, Jonah has walked 12,789 km of US backcountry. He joined Aurora Outfitters as a thru-hiker ambassador in 2022.

## Background

Jonah grew up in Albuquerque, started backpacking in scouts, and walked his first long trail (the PCT) in 2017 at age 22. He completed the AT in 2019 and the CDT in 2021 — three trails in five years. His brand voice is technical, candid, and impatient with marketing. He's the ambassador customers reach for when they want the unvarnished truth.

## What Jonah works on with Aurora Outfitters

- **Aurora Skyline 45L pack:** Jonah field-tested four prototype iterations on the 2024 CDT. The hip-belt V-shape, the brain pocket dimensions, and the load lifter angle all changed based on his data.
- **Aurora Quill 850 quilt:** Jonah's testing across desert and mountain phases of three thru-hikes drove the magnetic-snap footbox closure choice. (His feedback: "I want to throw it open in two seconds when I cowboy-camp, not fuss with a drawstring.")

## Jonah's gear philosophy

> "If a piece of gear can't survive 1,000 miles of continuous use, it doesn't get on my pack list. That's not a feature wishlist. That's the floor."

## Jonah's published Q&A

Jonah does a public Q&A on the Aurora Outfitters podcast every quarter. Recurring questions:

**Q: What's your base weight?**
**A:** 6.4 kg for shoulder season, 5.2 kg for summer. Aurora Skyline 45L is 920 g of that; the Aurora Quill 850 is 540 g; the Aurora Helios is 380 g; the rest is small.

**Q: What's the gear you've changed your mind about?**
**A:** Down quilts. I was a mummy-bag person for years; the Aurora Quill 850 converted me.

**Q: What's overrated?**
**A:** Most camp shoes. Bring crocs if you must; don't pay for trail-specific camp shoes.
