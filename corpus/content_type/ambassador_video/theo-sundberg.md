# Theo Sundberg — Climbing Instructor & Alpine Educator

Theo Sundberg has taught climbing for 22 years across the European Alps, Scotland, and the Dolomites. He holds the UIAGM/IFMGA mountain-guide certification and the Mountain Instructor Award (MIA — UK). His content for Aurora Outfitters focuses on skill-building, safety, and teaching customers to take their own decisions in the mountains.

## Background

Theo grew up near Innsbruck, started climbing at 7, qualified as a UIAGM guide at 31, and has run a climbing school out of Chamonix (France) since 2008. His client base is intermediate climbers learning multi-pitch and alpine skills.

## What Theo works on with Aurora Outfitters

- **Aurora Cumulus 2P tent:** Theo's climbing-school courses run multi-day in exposed alpine basins. The Aurora Cumulus 2P's storm-pole geometry was tested by his teams in 50+ km/h Pyrenean wind events; the brand kept his feedback at the centre of the geometry choice.
- **Hardshell development:** Theo is currently consulting on Aurora Outfitters' first technical hardshell, expected 2026.

## Theo on teaching customers

> "I disagree with the marketing line that good gear keeps you safe. Good *judgement* keeps you safe. Good gear keeps you comfortable while you exercise that judgement. The way Aurora Outfitters writes its product copy now — 'recommended for these conditions, not these' — that's the right model. It treats customers like grown adults."

## Theo's classes

Theo runs alpine-skills courses through his Chamonix outfit (Sundberg Alpine). Aurora Outfitters Trail Club Pro members get **20% off course fees** — see `16-loyalty-trail-club.md`.

Course catalogue:

- **Intro to Multi-Pitch** (3 days, May-Oct) — for climbers who can lead 5.8 indoors.
- **Crevasse Rescue Refresher** (1 day, May-Sep) — for any glacier traveller.
- **Alpine Trip Planning Weekend** (2 days, winter) — classroom + outdoor exercises.

## Theo's published safety positions

Theo posts a quarterly safety advisory through the Aurora Outfitters newsletter. Recurring themes:

- **Altitude and rest days.** Theo aligns with the standard rule: never sleep more than 500 m higher than the previous night above 3,000 m. See `22-altitude-acclimatisation.md`.
- **Beacon checks.** Mandatory before every winter outing.
- **Weather windows.** "If the forecast says four hours, plan for two."
