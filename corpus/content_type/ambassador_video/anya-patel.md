# Dr Anya Patel — Sports Physiologist & Gear-Fit Specialist

Dr Anya Patel is an Aurora Outfitters ambassador specialising in evidence-based gear selection for long-mile outdoor athletes. Anya's PhD is in sports physiology with a focus on lower-limb biomechanics. She joined the Aurora ambassador roster in 2025.

## What Anya works on with Aurora

- Boot-fit science. Anya's lab data drove the Wide Foot sizing option in the Aurora TerraTrek 7.
- Sleep-system thermoregulation studies. Anya's data informs the Aurora Quill 850 comfort-rating claims.
- Customer fit-consultation programme via the Trail Club Plus benefit.

## Customer access

- Trail Club Plus and Pro members can book a 30-minute fit consultation with Anya per year. Booking through the member portal.
- Trail Club Pro members get a 60-minute slot.

## Anya's public Q&A

Anya hosts a quarterly "Fit Science" webinar through Aurora. Recurring topics: foot swelling on multi-day trips, down-fill performance vs lab claims, sleeping-pad R-value reality vs spec.
