# Jonah Reyes Podcast — Episode 04

## "Triple Crown Gear Survival: What Lasts and What Doesn't"

Recorded 2027-11. 42 minutes. Jonah Reyes in conversation with Mara Chen and Theo Sundberg.

### Key takeaways

- Boot durability: Aurora TerraTrek 7 outperformed expectations on Jonah's CDT segment. ~1,000 km before resole needed.
- Pack durability: Aurora Skyline 45L hip-belt foam lasted ~3,500 km before showing meaningful compression. Replaced under Repair-for-Life.
- Quilt durability: Aurora Quill 850 down loft dropped ~12% over 4,800 km of consistent use. Workshop wash restored most of it.

### Mara on long-mile guiding

Mara discusses the difference between ambassador testing (representative miles) and long-mile thru-hiking. Different gear truths emerge after 1,000+ miles.

### Theo on alpine vs thru

Theo argues alpine days are higher-stakes per hour but lower-mileage. Different gear philosophy. Aurora's product line spans both reasonably.
